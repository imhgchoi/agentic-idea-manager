AIM HOMEPAGE — OPEN LOCALLY

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Open the extracted AIM_HOMEPAGE folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

Keep the folder contents together. You can move or rename the folder.
All page data, figures, and the paper are included and work offline.
No installation, server, API key, or original experiment files are needed.
Use a modern browser with JavaScript enabled.

README.md contains optional hosting and development instructions.
